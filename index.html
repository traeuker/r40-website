New: Kimi K3.1 Pro Recalls and Enforces Hidden Test Rules

Kimi K3.1 Pro is the top model on Artificial Analysis’s Terminal-Bench 4.0 leaderboard. In 23% of its attempts, its reasoning cites instructions that are not in the task but that the hidden tests check. Furthermore, in three of four tasks it ignores task edits that contradict these rules, which most likely indicates training on the benchmark tasks with a teacher that saw the hidden grading rules.

Update, September 2026: Adversaries Can Still Steal Reasoning from American Frontier Models via Third-Party Cloud Aggregators

Two months after our disclosure, we audit the mitigations deployed on the OpenAI and Anthropic APIs and on AWS Bedrock, Microsoft Azure, and OpenRouter. The patches are fragmented and reach third-party clouds with a delay of days. Reasoning replay still works on Azure for every OpenAI model, and scratchpad extraction still works for every OpenAI model, Opus 4.8, and Sonnet 5.

Recent work: Stealing Reasoning Traces from Proprietary LLM APIs

Anthropic, OpenAI, and Google return encrypted chain-of-thought blocks to clients, and these blocks can be replayed across sessions, users, and models. We replay a frontier model’s encrypted trace into a weaker model from the same provider, which then transcribes the hidden reasoning in plaintext. The attack never targets the stronger model directly and therefore does not trigger its anti-distillation safeguards.
